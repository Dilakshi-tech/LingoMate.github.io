Lesson 4: Passé Composé with Avoir

Language: French Pathway: G.C.E. O/L Level: Level 2

1. Learning Objectives

  • Understand how to conjugate past tenses using auxiliary verb 'avoir'.
  • Form past participles for regular -er, -ir, and -re verbs.
  • Construct daily past tense sentences with Sinhala/Tamil support.

2. Simple English & Sinhala Explanation

The Passé Composé is used to talk about completed past actions in French. In Sinhala, this corresponds to අතීත කාලය (Past Tense). It is formed by: Subject + Auxiliary (Avoir) + Past Participle.

Sinhala Support: ප්‍රංශ භාෂාවේ අතීත කාල වාක්‍ය සෑදීමට 'Avoir' ක්‍රියා පදය සහ අතීත කෘදන්තය (Past Participle) භාවිත කරයි.

Tamil Support: பிரெஞ்சு மொழியில் இறந்த கால வாக்கியங்களை உருவாக்க 'Avoir' வினைச்சொல் பயன்படுத்தப்படுகிறது.

3. Target Language Examples & Audio

J'ai mangé un croissant ce matin.

English: I ate a croissant this morning.

Sinhala: මම අද උදේ කෲසෝන් එකක් කෑවෙමි.
